## Chain-of-title rule

No item may be promoted to **VERIFIED RYAN OWNED** merely because it is in Ryan's GitHub repository, was paid for by Ryan/Dealworx, contains a copyright notice, or was generated with AI assistance. Promotion requires evidence supporting legal ownership or a valid written assignment where required.

## Corporate separation

Funding, development expenditure, repository access or commercial use by Dealworx must not be treated by RALPH as automatic evidence that Dealworx owns the underlying RALPH IP.

## Hard legal gate

Before material source disclosure to outside developers without adequate terms, external investor ownership rights, sale of RALPH IP, material third-party licensing, source-code transfer or material disclosure of confidential RALPH IP, require Ryan approval and appropriate South African IP/commercial legal review.
