# RALPH IP Legal Package — Drafting Brief

**DRAFTING BRIEF ONLY — NOT AN EXECUTED AGREEMENT — ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED**

Intended underlying IP owner: **Ryan Graham Donald**.

This brief is designed to give South African IP/commercial counsel a precise starting point. It must not be treated as an assignment, licence or legal opinion.

## A. Master IP Assignment / Confirmation

Objective: identify every person or entity that may own transferable rights in existing RALPH material and, where required, execute a written assignment vesting the intended rights in Ryan Graham Donald.

Counsel should address at least: definition of assigned IP; existing and future works only to the extent legally effective; copyright and related rights; software/source/object code; documentation/designs; inventions and know-how where applicable; improvements and derivative works; confidential information; warranties as to authority/title; disclosed third-party/open-source material; moral-rights treatment to the extent legally permissible; further assurances; consideration; governing law; signatures; effective dates; schedules identifying contributions and repositories.

Do not assume an assignor owns rights merely because they created, funded or possessed a copy of material.

## B. Ryan → Dealworx RALPH Licence

Objective: permit Dealworx to operate and commercialise RALPH while preserving Ryan's underlying ownership unless Ryan expressly agrees otherwise in a signed instrument.

Counsel should address: scope; field of use; territory; term; exclusivity/non-exclusivity; sublicensing; internal use; customer deployment; commercialisation; fees/royalties if any; improvements; derivative works; source-code access; data ownership and permitted data use; confidentiality; security; IP notices; enforcement and infringement; warranties/limitations; assignment; termination; survival; insolvency; change of control; sale of assets; equity transactions; shareholder exit; acquisition of Dealworx; treatment of improvements on termination; transition rights; no implied transfer of underlying RALPH IP.

Related-party and corporate-governance implications should be considered independently.

## C. Contributor IP Assignment / Confidentiality

Objective: ensure each employee, contractor, developer, designer, consultant, agency or technical partner contributing protectable material has appropriate written terms before material contribution.

Counsel should address: pre-existing materials; project contributions; future contributions to the extent legally effective; source code; documentation; UI/design; inventions; improvements; derivative works; confidential information; open-source disclosure/approval; third-party code restrictions; warranties of authority; further assurances; return/deletion of confidential material; access termination.

## D. Trade Secret / Confidentiality Controls

Identify material whose value depends on confidentiality, including unpublished architecture, economic logic, prompts, internal methods, security architecture, proprietary datasets, commercial methodology, credentials and deployment details. Contractual protections should align with technical least-privilege controls.

## E. Brand / Trademark Workstream

Review availability and registrability of `RALPH` and, where useful, `Ryan's Autonomous Logic & Profit Heuristic` in the relevant classes and territories. Do not claim registration until obtained.

- complete Git contributor history and material commit history;
- chain-of-title register;
- current and historical employment/contractor agreements relevant to contributors;
- any Dealworx documents describing RALPH ownership or platform status;
- any investor/customer/partner documents referring to RALPH rights;
